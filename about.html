What is hydraform technology?

Hydraform is a modern interlocking block construction system that uses hydraulically compressed earth and cement blocks. Blocks interlock without the need for mortar in vertical joints, making walls stronger, faster to build, and more thermally efficient.

Mortar use
Up to 40% reduction in mortar consumption
Build speed
Faster erection with fewer skilled-labour bottlenecks
Thermal performance
Excellent natural insulation
Environmental impact
Lower than fired brick
Wall strength
Consistent block geometry for stronger walls
